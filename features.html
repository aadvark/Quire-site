FEATURES

Everything Quire does.

A closer look at the reader that turns your favourite sites into magazines.

Front page Magazines Sites Discover Pages Reader Collections Control Offline
01 · FRONT PAGE

Your front page

Open Quire and the best of the day is waiting: the freshest articles, and a magazine for everything you follow.

Top stories

Swipe through every fresh article from the last six hours, from all your sites. Sites take turns, so a busy one can't crowd out the rest.

Today, This week or All

One switch at the top sets how far back Quire reaches, for top stories, issues and the new counts alike.

Counts that go down

An article you've already seen on a page stops counting as new, so the numbers reflect what's actually left.

Sources at a glance

Your sites sit in a row above your magazines, with Add first. Long-press one to remove it.

02 · MAGAZINES

A magazine for every topic

Every category you follow sites in becomes its own magazine on Home, with its own cover and look.

Real variety

Each round of pages takes one article from every site, in a different order each day. One busy site can't fill the issue.

Pages stay put

While an issue is open, its order holds steady. New articles join at the end instead of reshuffling what you've read.

Read to the end

Issues keep loading as you swipe, so every article is in reach, not just the newest.

See every site

Long-press a cover, or tap the sites button inside, for the full list. Tick several to remove them together.

Six looks

Give each category magazine a look of its own, from quiet editorial to bold.

03 · SITES

Bring any site

Follow the sites you actually read, from big newsrooms to small blogs, with or without a feed.

Paste an address

Quire looks for the site's feed. If there isn't one, it reads the site's map or the page itself.

Share from any app

Share a link to Quire from your browser or any other app. Save the article to read later, or follow the whole site.

Add several at once

Search, tick the results you want and add them together. Pick one category for all of them, or let Quire sort them.

A clear summary

After adding, Quire shows what was added, what you already follow, and what couldn't be added.

Healthy sources

Quire flags sites it can't reach. Check all sites fixes ones that moved, and offers to remove the rest in one go.

04 · DISCOVER

1,058 sites to discover

Not sure where to start? Every site in Discover has been checked for a working feed.

Pick topics on first open

Tap the topics you like and Quire follows good sites in each, so your first issue is ready straight away.

20 topics, 112 subtopics

From world news and technology to food, design and surfing, with plenty from Southeast Asia.

Ready-made issues

Starter packs laid out as magazine covers. Tap one to follow all its sites.

Select all

Inside any topic, tick the sites you want, or select them all, and follow them together.

05 · PAGES

Pages that read like a magazine

Articles are laid out as pages, not a feed. Each page picks a layout that suits its stories.

Ten layouts

Covers, features, mosaics, quotes and briefings. Every layout fills the page, with photos and headlines sized to fit.

Flip or slide

Pages fold over at the middle like a magazine, or slide sideways if you prefer. Choose under Aa.

Two-page spreads

On tablets and unfolded phones, issues open as spreads, two pages side by side.

06 · READER

A calm reader view

Tap any article for clean text in a serif made for long reading. The original is always one tap away.

Your theme

Day, Sepia, Dark, or follow your phone's setting.

Your text

Set the text size and choose the font you read best in.

Tidy articles

When a site repeats its headline at the top of an article, Quire drops it, and headings inside are sized to match.

Credit where it's due

Every article names its source and links back to the original.

07 · COLLECTIONS

Make your own magazines

Save any article, from any source, into a collection of your own.

Your cover

Use an article's photo, a plain colour or a picture of your own.

Edit freely

Rename a collection, take articles out one at a time or tick several together, with undo.

Delete when done

Remove a whole collection from inside it, or long-press its cover on Home.

08 · CONTROL

Read what you want

Quire is quiet by design, and you decide what reaches you.

Muted words

Add words or phrases you'd rather not see. Articles that mention them are hidden from issues, top stories and Latest.

Search everything

Search your saved articles, or every article on your phone.

No ads, ever

No ads, no account and no tracking. Nothing about your reading leaves your phone.

09 · OFFLINE

Offline, storage and backup

Quire keeps everything on your phone, and lets you choose how much.

Read without a connection

Issues download on Wi-Fi in the background, full text and cover photos included.

Choose how long to keep

Keep articles for a week, two weeks, a month or three months, and see how much space Quire uses.

Take your sites anywhere

Import and export your sites as OPML, which any feed reader understands.

Back up and restore

Save everything, settings included, to one file and bring it to a new phone.

Updates itself

Quire checks for new versions and installs them when you say so. Turn on Beta updates to try new things first.
