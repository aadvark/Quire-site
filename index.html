How it works

1

Paste a web address

A blog, a news site, a newsletter archive. Or share a link into Quire from any app.

2

Quire finds the articles

It looks for the site's feed, and if there isn't one, reads the site's map or the page itself.

3

Turn the pages

Big photos, varied layouts and clean article text, one swipe at a time.
