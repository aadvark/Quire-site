How it works

1

Pick topics, or bring your own sites

On first open, tap the topics you like and Quire follows good sites in each. Or paste any address: a blog, a news site, a newsletter archive.

2

Quire builds your magazines

It finds each site's articles, even without a feed, and mixes them into Top stories and a magazine for every topic you follow.

3

Flip through

Big photos, ten page layouts and clean article text. Pages flip up like a magazine, or slide sideways if you prefer.

YOUR FRONT PAGE

Top stories

Swipe through every fresh article from the last six hours, from all your sites, taking turns so no site crowds the others out.

A magazine for every topic

Each category you follow becomes its own magazine on Home, with its own look. Long-press a cover to see and edit its sites.

Real variety

Issues take one article from every site in turn, in a fresh order each day, so one busy site can't fill your magazine.

Today, This week or All

One switch sets how far back Quire reaches: just today's news, the week's, or everything it still has.

FINDING SITES

Bring any site

Follow the small sites you actually read, even ones with no RSS feed. Quire reads the site's map or the page itself.

Discover 1,058 sites

Browse 20 topics and ready-made issues, every site checked for a working feed. Tick several and add them in one go.

Share from any app

Share a link to Quire from your browser or any app, then save the article for later or follow the whole site.

Keep sources healthy

Quire flags sites that can't be reached, fixes ones that moved, and lets you remove the rest at once.

READING YOUR WAY

A calm reader view

Clean text in a serif made for long reading. Day, Sepia, Dark or System theme, your text size and font. The original is one tap away.

Flip or slide

Pages fold over at the middle like a magazine, or slide sideways. Tablets and unfolded phones show two-page spreads.

Muted words

Add words or phrases you'd rather not see, and articles that mention them are hidden everywhere.

Search everything

Search your saved articles, or every article on your phone.

Read offline

Issues download on Wi-Fi in the background. Choose how long articles are kept, from a week to three months.

Yours to take with you

Import and export your sites as OPML, and back up or restore everything, settings included.

Make your own magazines.

Give a collection a title and a cover, from an article's photo, a plain colour or your own picture. Add any article to it, from any source. Rename it, take out several articles at once, or delete it whenever you like.
