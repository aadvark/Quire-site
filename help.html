Questions, answered.

Getting Quire

How do I install Quire?

Quire is installed from this website rather than an app store.

  1. On your Android phone, tap Download the APK.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps.
  3. Tap Install, then Open.
Why isn't Quire on Google Play?

Not yet. Until it is, the APK here is the official version, signed the same way every release.

How do updates work?

You only install once. Quire checks for new versions itself and asks before installing. You can also check any time in Settings. Turn on Beta updates there to try new features early.

Which phones does it run on?

Any phone or tablet with Android 8.0 or newer. Tablets and unfolded phones show issues as two-page spreads.

Does it cost anything?

No. Quire is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Sites and articles

Can I follow a site that has no RSS feed?

Usually, yes. Quire looks for a feed first. If there isn't one, it reads the site's map or the page itself to find articles.

Why does an article show only part of the text?

Some sites put articles behind a paywall or build pages in a way Quire can't read yet. Tap through to the original to read it on the site. Sites in Discover that need a subscription for some articles are marked.

A site is flagged as unreachable. What now?

Open Settings and tap Check all sites. Quire tries every site, fixes any that moved their articles, and offers to remove the rest in one go.

Why do I see the same site less often now?

On purpose. Issues and top stories take one article from each site in turn, so a site that posts all day can't fill your magazine.

How do I hide topics I'm tired of?

Add muted words in Settings. Articles that mention them disappear from issues, top stories and Latest.

Storage and your data

How much space does Quire use?

You choose. In Settings › Offline, storage and backup, keep articles for a week, two weeks, a month or three months, and see how much space they take. Full text is kept for two weeks and fetched again if you open something older.

How do I move to a new phone?

Use Back up in Settings › Offline, storage and backup to save everything to one file, then Restore it on the new phone. To move only your sites, export them as OPML.

Does Quire collect my data?

No. There are no servers, accounts or analytics. Articles go straight from each site to your phone. Read the privacy policy.
