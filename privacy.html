The short version

Quire doesn't collect, store or send any personal data to its maker or anyone else. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

What stays on your phone

The sites you follow, the articles Quire downloads, your magazines, covers you upload and what you've read are stored only on your device. Uninstalling Quire deletes them.

What Quire connects to

To show you articles, Quire connects directly to the websites you add, the same way a browser does. Those sites can see an ordinary web request from your phone, including its IP address, and their own privacy policies apply. Quire doesn't route anything through its own servers, because it has none.

Children

Quire isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.
